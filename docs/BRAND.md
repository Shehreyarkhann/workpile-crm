# Workpile — Brand Identity v1 (2026-11-10)

## The mark
**The Pile.** Three rounded slabs stacked into a pyramid — the pile of work every
field-service business carries, stacked, ordered, and under control. The top slab is
Signal Green: the next job, always on top. The mark must read at 16px: three bars,
one green top. Never redraw it freehand; use `docs/brand/workpile-mark.svg`
(tile) or `workpile-mark-mono.svg` (bare glyph). Clear space = the height of one
slab on all sides. Minimum size 16px (tile) / 20px (lockup).

**Lockup:** tile + "Workpile" in Inter 800, letter-spacing −0.02em, Ink color
(wordmark in white on Pine/dark backgrounds).

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Pine | `#0E5C3A` | Primary brand, buttons, tile |
| Pine Deep | `#083B25` | Dark bands, footer, hero frame |
| Signal | `#22C55E` | The top slab, success, "go" accents |
| Signal Bright | `#4ADE80` | Top slab on the Pine tile only |
| Paper | `#F7F8F6` | Page background |
| Ink | `#101613` | Headlines |
| Slate | `#5B6B62` | Secondary text |
| Amber | `#D97706` | Attention states only (never decoration) |
| Risk | `#DC2626` | Overdue/danger only |

## Type
Inter (system fallbacks). Headlines 800, tight tracking (−0.03em), sentence case.
Body 400/1.6. Numbers in stats use 800. No other typefaces; no italics for emphasis
in headlines — use Signal color.

## Voice
Plain, concrete, owner's language. We name the work (leads, packets, proposals,
jobs, timesheets) — never "synergy", "leverage", or "solutions". Claims must be
product facts; metrics are labeled (target / estimate / measured) or not used.
Tagline: **"Turn every opportunity into accountable work."**
Descriptor: *The Revenue Execution OS for owner-operated field-service businesses.*

## Don'ts
- No invented customers, logos, testimonials, or metrics.
- No gradient meshes, stock photos, or cartoon illustration — real product
  screenshots in a Pine Deep frame, or typography.
- The mark never appears in Amber/Risk; attention colors are for states, not brand.
